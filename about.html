About RoyalDoors

A name that means an opening, not a gate kept shut.

Founded in 2019, DOT-accredited, and anchored today by a physical office in Binangonan, Rizal.

Who We Are

Budget-friendly travel, handled properly.

RoyalDoors is a Department of Tourism–accredited modern online travel and tour agency dedicated to providing hassle-free travel experiences and affordable tour packages and land arrangements.

We specialize in leveraging social media and personalized customer support to ensure our clients' travel needs are handled swiftly, efficiently, and professionally by our team of expert travel agents — for individuals, families, and corporate groups alike.

Our History

From an online-only agency to a storefront in Binangonan.

2019

RoyalDoors Travel and Tours begins as an online travel agency, with a simple mission: make travel accessible, hassle-free, and memorable for every traveler.

2019–2024

What started as an online agency grows into a trusted provider of affordable tour packages, land arrangements, and travel services for individuals, families, and corporate clients — through dedication, integrity, and partnerships with local and international tour operators.

2024

RoyalDoors opens its physical office at Tagpos Public Market, Binangonan, Rizal, giving clients a more convenient and personalized travel experience alongside the online service.

2026

Now Department of Tourism–accredited (DOT-R4A-TTA-02937-2026) and DTI-registered, RoyalDoors continues to grow as a trusted name in Philippine travel, guided by a vision of connecting people to destinations and experiences around the world.
