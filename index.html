About RoyalDoors

A name that means
an opening — not a gate kept shut.

RoyalDoors is a Department of Tourism–accredited online travel and tour agency dedicated to hassle-free travel experiences and affordable tour packages and land arrangements. We specialize in leveraging social media and personalized customer support so every inquiry is handled swiftly, efficiently, and professionally by our team of travel agents.

Founded in 2019 and now anchored by a physical office in Binangonan, Rizal, we plan trips for individuals, families, and corporate groups — from a weekend in El Nido to a first passport stamp in Taiwan.
